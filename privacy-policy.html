CutCount Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 28, 2026

In short
  • CutCount has no account and no server of its own. Your progress is saved only on your device.
  • The Android app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google collects some device data for ads, measurement and fraud prevention, subject to your consent where the law requires it.
  • Purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see your payment details.
  • You can change your ad privacy choices at any time in Settings > Privacy options.
  • CutCount is not directed to children under 13.

1. Who we are

CutCount ("the game", "we", "us") is a game made by Yoan BISOGNO EI, the creator of the CutCount ASMR videos (@cutcountasmr), 45 Rue Jean Moulin, 78400 Chatou, France. This policy applies to the CutCount app for Android and to the web version of the game. For any question about privacy, contact us at CutCountASMR@gmail.com. Our full details are in the legal notice.

2. Data stored on your device

The game saves your progress locally on your device: level and XP, coins, unlocked fruits, knives and studio looks, best scores, daily rewards and missions, settings (sound, haptics, quality) and whether you own "Remove Ads", the "Starter Pack" or the "Studio" (and when you last tried the Studio). On Android it keeps a second copy in the app's own storage, so that a cleared browser storage does not lose it. This data never leaves your device through us: we have no server and we cannot see it.

It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data (on the web: when you clear the site data of your browser). If your Android device backup is turned on, Android may include this data in your device backup, which is managed by Google under your Google account settings.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Android app shows ads served by Google AdMob: rewarded ads that you choose to watch for a bonus, and ads between some rounds. To serve and measure these ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK built into the app collects and shares the following data with Google:

DataExamplesPurposes
Device and advertising identifiersAndroid advertising ID, app set ID Advertising, analytics, fraud prevention
Approximate locationDerived from your IP address (not your precise GPS location) Advertising, analytics, fraud prevention
App interactionsApp launches, taps on ads, video ad views Advertising, analytics, fraud prevention
Diagnostics and device informationDevice model, operating system, ad SDK performance Advertising, analytics, fraud prevention

This data is sent over encrypted connections. Google uses it as described in its own policies. Learn how Google uses information from sites and apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

We do not receive your name, email address or any data that directly identifies you. We do not sell your personal data.

4. Your consent and your choices

  • European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland: before any personalised ads are shown, the app displays a consent message provided by Google (User Messaging Platform). You can accept, refuse or choose by purpose. If you refuse, you may still see non-personalised or limited ads.
  • United States: where state privacy laws apply (for example California), the app offers a "Do Not Sell or Share" choice through the same Google message.
  • Change your choice at any time: open the game's Settings > Privacy options.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads, the path may vary by device).
  • Remove Ads: buying "Remove Ads" or the "Starter Pack" stops the ads between rounds. Rewarded ads stay available only if you choose to watch them.

5. In-app purchases (Google Play)

Purchases ("Remove Ads", "Starter Pack", "Studio", coin packs) are processed entirely by Google Play. Your payment details are handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy; we never see them. The game only receives from Google Play the status of your purchase (which product, whether it is completed) so it can deliver the item and restore permanent purchases with Settings > Restore purchases. This status is stored on your device.

6. Notifications

If you allow it, the app schedules a daily reminder on your device (around 7 pm local time). The reminder is created and stored on your device only: no data is sent to us. You can turn notifications off at any time in your Android settings.

7. Sharing and links

  • When you share your score, the game opens your device's share menu with a short text and a link. Nothing is sent unless you choose an app and send it yourself.
  • "Share your cut" films the game's own picture and sound during a round (never the camera or the microphone). The clip is made on your device, kept in the app's temporary storage, and only leaves it when you choose an app in the share menu and send it yourself.
  • The "Watch the videos" button opens our YouTube channel in YouTube or your browser. YouTube's own terms and privacy policy then apply.
  • When the app asks you to rate it, the rating screen is provided by Google Play.

8. Web version (CrazyGames)

On the web version published on CrazyGames, ads and any account features are provided by CrazyGames and its partners, under the CrazyGames privacy policy. The game itself saves your progress in your browser's local storage, or with the storage provided by CrazyGames.

9. Analytics

Apart from the ad measurement described in section 3, the game does not use any separate analytics or tracking tool. If we add one, we will update this policy and the app's privacy information first.

When it starts, the game downloads a small settings file from its website (cutcountasmr.github.io, hosted by GitHub Pages), so that values such as reward amounts and how often ads appear can be adjusted without an update. This request sends nothing about you or your progress. As with any web page, GitHub receives your IP address to deliver the file and may keep it briefly in its server logs, under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

10. Children

CutCount is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the app and that Google has collected data about them through it, contact us at CutCountASMR@gmail.com and we will help.

11. Data retention and your rights

We do not hold any personal data on our side: there is nothing for us to keep, correct or delete. The data stored on your device stays until you uninstall the app or clear its data. Data collected by Google for ads is kept and handled by Google under its policies; you can manage it through the controls above and your Google account.

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or state laws in the United States), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, to object to or restrict its use, and to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority. To exercise these rights or ask any question, write to CutCountASMR@gmail.com.

12. Security

The game does not transmit your progress anywhere. The Google services built into the app (ads and purchases) use encrypted connections.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when the game gets a new feature. The new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
